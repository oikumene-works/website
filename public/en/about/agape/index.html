Agape

Agape (Greek: ἀγάπη) means love. The word itself does not restrict love to any one particular kind.

In the New Testament, the word is used, among other things, for God's love for human beings and for the love people have for one another. Paul describes love that does not seek its own interests. In Jesus' teaching, love and doing good extend even to those who do not return that love.

Sources

Liddell–Scott–Jones, A Greek-English Lexicon, entry ἀγάπη; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Luke 6:27–36; 1 John 4:7–19.

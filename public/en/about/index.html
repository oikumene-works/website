About the project

Christian Belief Explorer is an experimental project being developed openly.

What is being developed?

The questions aim to help people clarify their own thinking. Comparisons refer to named views and sources; they are not judgments of a person’s faith.

The first pilot concerns the Bible and interpretive authority. The questions and comparisons are still experimental.

The intended service is bilingual, with questions, explanations and sources reviewed separately in each language.

About Oikumene

Oikumene is a website with a Christian foundation that brings together a variety of projects. Projects by people other than the site’s maintainer may also be included.

Guiding principles

A central value across the projects is to benefit users without asking for anything in return, regardless of their convictions or worldview.

The site does not market or promote individuals, traditions, denominations, doctrines or similar subjects.

For research, the aim is to provide information as free from distortion as possible. We seek to make sources, interpretations and significant uncertainties visible.

Drawing conclusions is left to readers themselves whenever possible.

Use of AI

Artificial intelligence is also used in developing projects. Its role is disclosed particularly when this matters for assessing the origin of the material, the research method or its reliability.
